# Strategy Lab data admission plan

**MANUAL_OUTSIDE_LAB · No validated model.** Historical admission requires a permitted development dataset, dated economic evidence, an explicit availability policy, and chronological warmup, development and validation windows. The full local inventory remains with the source receipts and study records.

## Admission decision

Position can use a continuous independently approved BTC-USDT Spot trade archive with dated instrument terms. Day and Swing require independently approved futures trade and mark observations, complete actual funding settlements, and dated contract and margin rules. A public endpoint or an existing local file does not establish permission for a new research purpose.

## Inherited permissions and exclusions

Keep sealed holdouts and prospective evidence from Phases 14 and 15 outside Strategy Lab development intake. Phase 17 and 17.1 have their own source and use boundaries. A feature-only or coverage-only grant does not authorize economic replay. Preserve the original provider receipts, normalize only an eligible export, and record the export hash and approved scope. [Phase 17 and 17.1 runbook](PHASE17_RUNBOOK.md)

## Position intake and first replay

Use complete UTC hourly Spot candles to build daily decisions. Include enough approved prehistory for the frozen strategy and the previous-bar signal checks. Verify the native time grid, instrument rules throughout the export, explicit fee and adverse-execution assumptions, and availability evidence. A reviewed historical close-time proxy remains an assumption.

## Futures intake specification

Require matching UTC trade and mark minute grids, actual settled funding and its dated schedule, the contract multiplier, tick and lot sizes, and applicable margin and liquidation rules. Native trade, mark and funding receipts serve different evidence roles. Leave missing settlement or mark observations explicit. Document economic and causal limitations before replay.

Inspect the current command reference and prepare the approved external bundle before calling the admission or replay commands. The foreground runner has separate memory, compute-time and disk guards. [Strategy Lab runbook](STRATEGY_LAB_RUNBOOK.md)
