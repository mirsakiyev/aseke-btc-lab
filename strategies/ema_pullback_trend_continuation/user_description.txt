The strategy attempts to enter during trend continuation
after a retracement toward the 50 EMA, rather than entering
on a range breakout.

The 50 EMA serves as the pullback reference level.
The 200 EMA provides a broader trend filter.

After a previous candle touches/crosses the fast EMA,
a completed confirmation candle must close back on
the appropriate side of it.

The confirmation candle must have above-average volume
and sufficient RSI momentum.

The trade uses a structural stop at the rolling five-bar
extreme, with a 0.5 ATR buffer, and a target 2.5 ATR
away from the confirmation candle close.

The author's narrative suggests the strategy may
perform better in trending markets than ranging markets.
This is a hypothesis, not an established performance fact.

Suggested research profiles:
- Swing: KuCoin XBTUSDTM, 4-hour candles
- Position: KuCoin BTC-USDT Spot, daily candles
- Day: possible later 15-minute diagnostic candidate

Prioritize Swing and Position initially.

Use the same indicator periods in native candles for
each timeframe. Do not silently reinterpret 50 EMA
as a different number of bars.
